What this means in plain words

  • Online booking clears demand against real open slots.
  • Reception phones become freer for real conversations.

How BookMyClinics helps

Patients who can self-serve book online. Your staff keep the line for everything else. Not “no phones ever” — just phones no longer being the only door.
