What this means in plain words

The cost of booking is mostly people answering phones. When online booking takes the routine slots, clinics need fewer hours of pure scheduling labor — sometimes around 20 hours a week saved on booking admin in published digital programs. There is a second cost too: paying staff through empty sessions. When unused slots fall, your team’s time is spent on real patients.

  • Less phone booking work means lower admin cost per appointment.
  • Fewer empty sessions mean better value from staff already on duty.
  • Online booking scales without adding more phone lines.
